Welcome to QRT

Getting around
  * The dock holds your pinned and open apps. The dots at its end open the
    list of all apps, with a search. Drag the dock to any edge of the screen.
  * Swipe up on the home screen to see your open apps. Tap one to switch to
    it; tap its x, or swipe it up, to close it.
  * Every app window has minimise and close buttons at the top right.
  * Tap a text field to bring up the on-screen keyboard.

Buttons
  * Power: lock the screen. Again: sleep. Hold for a second: power menu.
  * Volume up and down: volume.
  * Windows button: the list of all apps.

Apps
  * Files          - browse the boot drive
  * Terminal       - a command line for Linux programs (busybox, ls, wget...)
  * Browser        - web pages over http:// and https://
  * Wi-Fi          - connect to a wireless network
  * Settings       - accent colour, rotation, sleep, brightness, volume
  * System Monitor - processor, memory, network and hardware
  * Clock, Drawing
